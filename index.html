Our Signature Skill

A Blend for Every Season

Tobacco doesn't just behave differently with Gujarat's changing humidity and heat — its moisture behaviour and perceived strength both shift with the season. A mixture calibrated for peak summer can feel far too strong, or far too weak, once winter sets in. Balancing moisture and strength together, season after season, is the expertise our grandfather and father built over decades — and it's what keeps our customers on a reliable delivery schedule instead of guessing.

Summer

March – June

In the dry heat, we calibrate the mixture toward a milder strength profile, while managing moisture so it doesn't turn brittle before it reaches the roller.

Whatever the season, deliveries go out on a regular, agreed schedule — so our customers plan production around us, not around uncertainty.

Where It Comes From

Two Soils, One Signature Blend

A dependable bidi mixture rarely comes from one growing region alone. We bring together leaf from two very different tracts of India to build a mixture with both consistency and character.

Home Ground

Kheda–Anand Belt, Gujarat

Our own backyard — the tobacco-growing tract along the Mahisagar and Vatrak rivers in Kheda and Anand districts, often called Gujarat's "golden leaf" country. This is where our farmer network grows the bidi tobacco that forms the body of every mixture we make.
